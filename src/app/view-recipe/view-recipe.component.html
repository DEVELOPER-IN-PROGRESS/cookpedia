Brazilian Caipirinha

Cuisine:Brazilian
Serving:1
Mode of cooking:Easy

Preperation Time:

5 minutes

Cooking Time:

0 minutes

Calories per Serving:

150 minutes

Ingredients Needed

  1. Cachaca(Brazilian sugarcane spiril)
  2. Lime, cut into wedges
  3. Granulated sugar
  4. Ice cubes

Cooking Instruction

  • In a glass muddle lime wedges with granulated sugar to release the juice
  • Fill the glass with ice cubes
  • Pour cachaca over the ice and stir well
  • sip and enjoy the refreshing taste of the Brazilian caripirinha!
  • Adjust sugar and lime to suit your taste preference
